Photography Booking Information

Booking Form

I am available to shoot a range of subjects including events, fashion, creative projects, and weddings in either digital or film formats.

Interested clients can please reach out to me describing your project with example images/mood board/shot list. Please look through my portfolio for examples of my past work, though you can send me anything for inspiration. Below are the packages I am currently offering.

Digital Package: $150 digital. A 60 minute session at a single location. After completion of the session I will provide 20 edited images in an online gallery with a print release. I can provide smaller resolution copies of the photos for easier use in social media at the client's request.

Film Package: $220. A 60 minute session at a single location. After completion of the session I will provide 20 edited images in an online gallery with a print release. I can provide smaller resolution copies of the photos for easier use in social media at the client's request.

Combo Package: $320 digital/film combo. A 60 minute session at a single location. I will shoot both digital and film, and provide 15 edited photos of each format in an online gallery with print release.

Cyanotype Prints: $50 per print. Includes a one of a kind, hand made cyanotype of any image taken from your session or any image from my personal portfolio. Available to mail upon request.

Discounts and Deals!

Additional Sessions: 20% off if you wish to book a follow up session within two weeks.

Student Projects: Projects with merit will recieve a 20-100% discount on all digital services, up to my discretion. Reach out with your ideas!

Referrals: New clients referred by a past client will recieve 15% off, and the past client will recieve 15% off their next session.

Headshots: Headshots for those in need are totally free. Please contact through the booking link and describe your situation.
